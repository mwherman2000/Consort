Plant-based swaps for the Greek-Style Snack Wrap:

1. Eggs (scrambled): Soft tofu (crumbled/mashed) or a commercial liquid egg replacer (like JUST Egg). Season and scramble the same way for similar soft curds and savory bite.
2. Tzatziki sauce: Dairy-free tzatziki (store-bought or homemade with plain coconut or soy yogurt, grated cucumber, garlic, lemon, dill/mint). Closest creamy, tangy, herby match.
3. Feta cheese: Crumbled dairy-free feta (almond-, tofu-, or coconut-based brands). Salty, briny, crumbly texture and tang.

Keep the olive oil, spinach, oregano, salt/pepper, tortillas, and tomatoes as-is (most flour tortillas are already plant-based). Warm the tortillas first for easy rolling.
